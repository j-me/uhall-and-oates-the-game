OLD ORCHARD BEACH, MAINE · 1986

OUT OF TOUCH
IN O.O.B.

Uhall & Oates moves what matters. Mostly furniture. Occasionally the entire burden of a two-person business.

At a packed pier promotion, John Oates does the lifting while Daryl Hall supplies a heroic jingle and avoids eye contact with every box.

Then Jesse and Joe Reardon arrive with a contract-shaped trap, a suspicious moving truck, and a fake “New Hit Record” crate. Daryl disappears inside it.

Joe Timmins still expects every sofa delivered on time. John has an empty tape roll, one bad lead, and a missing partner to find.
